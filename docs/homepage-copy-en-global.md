# FreightVanta English / Global — Homepage Copy & Section Specification

> Purpose: the global English homepage for US-oriented B2B buyers. The copy is original, direct and operational. It keeps the shared business facts—sourcing, freight, customs, storage and fulfillment—without promising unverified transit times, savings or coverage numbers.

## 1. Page positioning

- Audience: ecommerce operators, importers, product teams and distributors moving goods between Asia, North America and other markets.
- Voice: direct, calm, accountable; explain the next action instead of using logistics jargon.
- Primary conversion: request a shipment plan.
- Secondary conversion: browse services and read practical guides.

## 2. Navigation

`Solutions` (mega menu: Product sourcing, Ocean freight, Air freight, Ground & final mile, Customs coordination, Warehousing & fulfillment) · `Industries` · `Insights` · `About` · language switcher · `Get a shipment plan`.

## 5. Operational advantages

**H2:** The details are where reliability is built.

1. **A named point of contact** — One person keeps the request, supplier questions and fulfillment notes in the same thread.
2. **Quality checks before dispatch** — Confirm product condition, quantity and agreed packaging instructions before goods leave the facility.
3. **Flexible shipment preparation** — Consolidate orders, separate destinations or hold inventory according to the plan you approve.
4. **Trackable handoffs** — Keep carrier references and status notes together so your team knows what changed and what happens next.

## 6. How it works

**H2:** A practical process for complex freight.

- **01 / Tell us what is moving:** product links, quantities, destination, target date and any packaging or compliance needs.
- **02 / Review the plan:** we clarify the service path, open questions, documents and responsibilities before work starts.
- **03 / Coordinate every handoff:** sourcing, purchase, inspection, preparation, transport and delivery updates follow one record.
- **04 / Keep your customer promise:** receive the tracking and exception notes needed to plan inventory and customer communication.

**Process reassurance:** If an assumption changes, the plan should show the change instead of hiding it in a long email chain.

## 9. FAQ

- **Can I start with only a product link?** Yes. A link, photo or short specification is enough for an initial sourcing conversation; quantities and destination can follow.
- **Can you combine sourcing and fulfillment?** The homepage should present them as one coordinated workflow, with the final scope confirmed in the shipment plan.
- **Do you provide customs clearance?** We coordinate the information and handoffs; the site must identify the responsible broker and jurisdiction before making a clearance claim.
- **Can I request a single service?** Yes. The form lets visitors select sourcing, transport, storage, branding, fulfillment or a combined plan.
