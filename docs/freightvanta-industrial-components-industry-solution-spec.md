# FreightVanta Industrial Components Industry Solution

## Purpose

The third live English global-site industry route is `/industries/industrial-components`. It is written for teams moving specification-led industrial parts from China into production, maintenance, project-site, consolidation, or freight-release handoffs. It separates logistics coordination from engineering approval, inspection, testing, regulatory, warehouse, carrier, and site-delivery responsibilities that need actual confirmation.

## Narrative And Module Order

1. Hero: active component specification to a controlled receiving handoff; `REFERENCE -> PACK -> DOCUMENTS -> RECEIVE -> RELEASE`.
2. Decision framing: a correct part still needs a usable receiving decision.
3. Six-step workflow: active reference, China purchase handoff, packing/handling, document path, inbound identification, destination release.
4. Component contexts: production input, maintenance/spares, and project/site delivery.
5. Operating controls: visible coordination work versus scope requiring confirmation.
6. Receipt context: revision, protective method, evidence, documents, destination, and receiving owner.
7. Planning brief: the active reference, quantity, destination, packing, documents, timing, and decision owners.
8. FAQ: six native disclosure rows covering engineering, packing, handling, and delivery boundaries.
9. Related services and final CTA: sourcing, bulk procurement, inventory storage, and packaging rows followed by a calm conversion band.
