# FreightVanta Consumer Goods Industry Solution

## Purpose

The second live English global-site industry route is `/industries/consumer-goods`. It is written for teams moving consumer goods from China into retail, wholesale, DTC, seasonal, replenishment, or freight-release handoffs. It does not present those contexts as one generic service or promise unverified quality, compliance, warehouse, delivery, or channel capability.

## Narrative And Module Order

1. Hero: China sourcing to a retail-ready or customer-ready handoff; `PRODUCT -> CHECKS -> PACKOUT -> INBOUND -> RELEASE`.
2. Decision framing: a consumer product is more than the item inside the carton.
3. Six-step workflow: approved reference, China sourcing handoff, evidence rule, presentation, inbound context, route release.
4. Release contexts: DTC/subscription, retail/wholesale, and seasonal/campaign.
5. Operating controls: a visible split between coordination work and scope that needs confirmation.
6. Product-context checklist: product version, packout rule, destination, receipt evidence, and release owner.
7. Planning brief: concise request inputs that start with known facts and retain unknowns.
8. FAQ: six accessible native disclosure rows that establish boundaries before an enquiry.
9. Related services and final CTA: linked editorial rows, then a clear conversion band.

## SEO Direction

- Primary phrase: `consumer goods logistics from China`
- Supporting phrases: `consumer goods sourcing and packaging from China`, `China consumer goods supply chain coordination`, `quality and packaging coordination from China`, `retail-ready consumer goods handoff`
- Required visible context: China source, product version, packaging or presentation, inbound inventory, retail / wholesale / DTC route, and confirmation boundaries.
- JSON-LD: `WebPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage`; do not emit `hreflang` before localized versions exist.

## Visual Direction

Use the English FreightVanta industry-page composition rather than a new card system: deep navy hero, white editorial decision section, cool-blue connected workflow, structured scenario rows, navy scope controls, image-led product-context split, pale-blue request, FAQ, related services, and CTA. Use existing project photography for physical consumer goods and packout work; no stock claims, visualized tracking data, map globes, decorative line art, or fabricated operational metrics.

## Scope Guardrails

Product quality, inspection, testing, supplier selection, product compliance, handling capability, delivery timing, tracking, rates, return handling, storage availability, retailer requirements, and market regulations must remain framed as subject to actual confirmed scope. The page can organize the questions and working handoffs; it must not manufacture an outcome or capability.
