General Rate is the base duty rate

It is not the total duty payable. Country of origin, Chapter 99 measures such as Section 301, trade remedies, fees and legal notes may change the final amount. A “Free” General rate does not mean all import charges are zero. Confirm classification and applicable measures before filing an entry.

How to read a result

The description comes from the official schedule. Open its legal context to see parent headings and any inherited notes. When a rate belongs to a parent heading, its source code appears below the rate.

Units are the schedule’s reporting units, such as No., kg or doz.. A missing rate is shown as “See schedule”, not assumed to be zero.

Data source and updates

This tool uses a local copy of the US International Trade Commission’s HTS. It checks for updates monthly and keeps the previous verified version if a download fails.

Keyword matches help you find entries to review. They are not a binding classification or an official customs ruling.
