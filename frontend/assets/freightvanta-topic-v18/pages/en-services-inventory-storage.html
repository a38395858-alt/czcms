INVENTORY STORAGE

Give imported inventory a clear receiving, storage, and release plan.

When goods arrive from China, the next question is not only where they sit. The team needs to know what arrived, which product version it belongs to, how it is identified, what evidence is recorded, what may be released, and who decides when a count, carton, label or destination does not match. FreightVanta helps organize that operating record before storage and release scope is confirmed.

Page flow: notice, then receive, then record, then purpose, then release.

Page flow

  1. 01 / NOTICE

    Expected inbound and documents

  2. 02 / RECEIVE

    Identify cartons and version

  3. 03 / RECORD

    Count, condition, evidence

  4. 04 / PURPOSE

    Hold, store, release owner

  5. 05 / RELEASE

    To freight, retail, or fulfillment

Storage is useful when the next release decision is visible.

A warehouse address or a stock count alone does not tell the next team whether goods are sellable, reserved, awaiting evidence, intended for a retailer, or held for a later fulfillment order. A receiving-to-release record connects product identity, quantity, packaging, destination and approval owner.

Receiving-to-release workflow

  1. Step 01

    Prepare expected inbound notice and document context.

  2. Step 02

    Receive and identify cartons, pallets, products and active version.

  3. Step 03

    Record count, visible condition and evidence required by confirmed scope.

  4. Step 04

    Assign stock purpose, hold status, storage instruction and release owner.

  5. Step 05

    Connect packaging, label, bundle or destination rule.

  6. Step 06

    Release to confirmed freight, retailer, fulfillment or another nominated handoff.

  7. Step 07

    Escalate discrepancy, damage, missing document or quantity difference for a decision.

When it fits

  1. 01

    GOODS ARE ARRIVING BEFORE THE FINAL RELEASE

    Record expected cartons, product identity and hold/release rule.

  2. 02

    ONE INBOUND FEEDS MULTIPLE DESTINATIONS

    Separate retail, freight, replenishment and order-preparation purpose.

  3. 03

    INVENTORY INFORMATION IS SCATTERED

    Unite SKU/reference, count, images, damage notes and open decisions.

  4. 04

    PACKAGING OR BRAND MATERIALS ARE STORED WITH THE PRODUCT

    Keep component and packout versions visible.

Preparation checklist

  • Expected inbound date or period.
  • Cartons/pallets.
  • Product and SKU list.
  • Supplier and origin.
  • Active packaging/version.
  • Destination or stock purpose.
  • Count and evidence requirement.
  • Release owner.
  • Special handling or restriction to review.

Inventory storage: questions worth answering early.

01Can you store products imported from China?

Share origin, product, quantity, timing and destination; actual location, capacity and handling must be confirmed.

02Can inventory be released in stages?

Yes, when stock purpose, identification and approval owner are defined.

03Do you guarantee a count or inspection result?

No; evidence, inspection and acceptance standards require explicit scope.

04Can packaging and inserts remain with the inventory?

They can be included in an approved component and release rule.

05Can storage connect to fulfillment?

Yes, once sellable identity, packing rule and order handoff are ready.

06What happens if cartons do not match the notice?

Record the discrepancy and route it to the named decision owner before release.
