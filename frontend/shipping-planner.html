Six steps from the Chinese supplier to the US address

  1. 01

    Supplier pickup

    Confirm the cargo is ready, the loading conditions at the factory, and the pickup window. This is the first point where a delay changes every later date.

  2. 02

    Export clearance

    Export declaration, commodity details, and the cut-off that matches the booked sailing or flight. Documents are checked against the booking before the container closes.

  3. 03

    Ocean or air leg

    The main carriage, with a sailing or flight you can check against a schedule rather than an estimate. Port or airport pairs follow the lane you selected above.

  4. 04

    US customs entry

    ISF is filed at least 24 hours before loading; the entry is prepared before arrival. HTS classification drives both duty and any Section 301 additional tariff.

  5. 05

    Port to door

    Container release, devanning where the cargo is consolidated, and a delivery appointment that matches the receiver's hours and equipment.

  6. 06

    Delivery and proof

    Signed proof of delivery, exceptions recorded, and the documents archived so the next shipment starts from a known state.

China to US shipping: frequently asked questions

How long does ocean freight from China to the US take?

Standard ocean service runs about 30–40 days door to door to the US West Coast and 37–53 days to the East Coast, depending on the origin port and routing. Premium services to Los Angeles are shorter. All figures exclude booking and cargo preparation time.

What does it cost to ship a 40HQ container from China?

Reference ranges in the table above run from roughly USD 3,100 to 5,000 for a 40HQ depending on destination coast and service level. Port fees, drayage, duty and tax are separate, so compare quotes on the same scope.

Is LCL or FCL cheaper for my volume?

Below roughly 15 CBM, LCL is usually cheaper because you pay per cubic metre. Above that, the fixed cost of a container is spread over enough volume that FCL wins, and it removes the consolidation wait.

What is ISF and when must it be filed?

The Importer Security Filing (10+2) must be submitted at least 24 hours before the cargo is loaded onto the vessel. A late or missing filing can trigger penalties, so it is treated as a hard deadline rather than a document to complete on arrival.

Can you handle customs and delivery, not just the ocean leg?

Yes. The calculator covers door-to-door scope: pickup, export clearance, main carriage, US customs entry and delivery, or a US warehouse to final address leg for inventory already in the country.
