The details before the order shape everything after it.

Sourcing becomes difficult when the product reference, supplier questions, packaging expectations, and delivery plan live in different conversations. A clear brief gives every next owner the same starting point.

We turn the early product conversation into a practical working document: what is known, what needs confirmation, and what should not move forward until your team has approved it.

When product sourcing is the right starting point.

Use sourcing coordination when the opportunity is clear, but the information required for a confident supplier conversation is still scattered, incomplete, or not yet comparable.

  1. 01 / REFERENCE

    You have a product reference but not a supplier-ready brief.

    Bring a product link, photo, competitor reference, or rough specification. We identify the material, size, finish, quantity, packaging, destination, and decision questions still needed before a supplier can give a useful answer.

  2. 02 / OPTIONS

    You need to compare options before committing.

    Use the service when supplier responses need to be arranged into an understandable comparison instead of living across disconnected chat messages and spreadsheets. Differences and gaps remain visible while your team decides.

  3. 03 / HANDOFF

    The next stage depends on product details.

    Start here when samples, packaging, inspection, freight, storage, or fulfillment cannot be planned responsibly until product information is consistent and an approved direction is documented.

A sourcing workflow that keeps decisions visible.

Each step creates a cleaner handoff for the next one. Questions stay open until there is an answer, and approval stays with the team making the purchase decision.

01

Share the starting reference.

Provide links, images, specifications, expected quantity, destination, and the outcome you are trying to achieve. Missing details are recorded as questions, not guessed.

02

Build the sourcing brief.

Product attributes, desired variations, target packaging, timing, and decision criteria are organized into a supplier-facing request.

03

Collect comparable answers.

Supplier responses are grouped by the questions they actually address, making product differences and missing information easier to review.

04

Review samples or evidence.

Photos, videos, physical samples, and supplier statements are inputs for approval. Your team decides what meets the brief.

05

Prepare the next handoff.

Approved product details, quantities, packaging needs, and receiving instructions are prepared for the next operational owner.

Common questions before a sourcing conversation.

The answers below set realistic expectations about coordination, approval, and how sourcing can connect to later service steps.

Can we start with only a product link?

Yes. A product link, image, or short description is enough to start an initial conversation. FreightVanta will identify the missing details needed to turn it into a usable brief.

Do you guarantee the supplier or product quality?

No quality, factory, price, or compliance guarantee should be assumed from a sourcing conversation. The service coordinates questions, comparisons, evidence, and approval steps; specialist services and acceptance standards need to be confirmed separately.

Can sourcing connect to fulfillment?

Yes. Once the product, packaging, quantity, and intended destination are approved, the handoff can connect to inventory storage, packaging, freight, and fulfillment planning.

What happens after an option is selected?

The chosen product details, quantities, packaging requirements, and receiving instructions can be prepared for the next operational owner. The next step depends on the service plan your team approves.
