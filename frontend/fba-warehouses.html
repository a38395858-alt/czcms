Directory coverage, coordinates & sources

This independent directory covers {{.FBAWarehouse.Locations}} selected FBA reference addresses across {{.FBAWarehouse.StateCount}} states. It is not a complete or live Amazon facility register. US West, Central and East are FreightVanta planning groups.

Addresses: FBA Near Me, with conflicting entries reviewed against Warehouse Ninja. Public address pages checked {{.FBAWarehouse.CheckedAt}}.

Location search and map reference points use US Census geocoding and 2025 Gazetteer place / ZIP areas. Street searches are sent to US Census, with © OpenStreetMap / Nominatim as a fallback. Street points are address-range estimates or approximate public map references; ZIP and city points are area centers. None identify a warehouse gate. Distances are straight-line estimates, not driving distances or delivery times.

Third-party examples demonstrate service filters and are not verified operators, partners or available facilities. No registered 3PL listings are available yet. Always confirm your receiving address and appointment against the Amazon shipment plan before dispatch.

Map boundaries: US Atlas / US Census. Map rendering: D3 and TopoJSON.
